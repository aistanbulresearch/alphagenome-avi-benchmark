# Figure captions (draft)

**Figure 1. Study design.**
Part 1 uses ClinVar classifications first made after the AlphaGenome training snapshot (2025-06-15); thresholds for clinical evidence are derived on a separate calibration sample of earlier labels and tested on the main set. Part 2 uses experimental splicing measurements in which no predictor selected the variants. Counts are variants (Part 1) or SNV assay variants with the number measured as splice-altering (Part 2).

**Figure 2. Discrimination on ClinVar labels added after the AlphaGenome training snapshot (Part 1).**
(a) ROC curves for intronic variants at least 3 bases from an exon in the main set (449 P/LP, 28,188 B/LB); false positive rate on a log scale to show the high-specificity range. Pangolin was run on all P/LP and a region-stratified sample of 4,000 B/LB; its curve and AUROC use inverse-sampling weights, and its curve is stepped at low false positive rates because fewer B/LB were scored. phyloP, 100-way vertebrate phyloP. (b) Paired AUROC differences, AVI minus each tool, on the variants both tools score; the main comparison (all intronic variants ≥3 bp) is shown first. Intervals are 95% gene-cluster bootstrap intervals (2,000 replicates).

**Figure 3. Calibration as clinical evidence (Part 1).**
(a) Thresholds for each evidence level were derived on the calibration sample with the ClinGen method of Pejaver et al. (2022), prior 0.0441, and tested on the main set (non-canonical intronic and UTR variants), each level within its own score interval. Points are interval likelihood ratios with 95% gene-bootstrap intervals. Where a derivation window or a test interval holds no B/LB (or no P/LP), the bootstrap cannot vary the empty class, and exact Clopper–Pearson bounds of the two proportions are used instead; the rule is the same for every tool. With it, no tool reaches the very strong level, and the strong interval is open-ended. Vertical bars: ClinGen targets (2.406, 5.790, 33.53 and 1,124; benign levels the reciprocals). A level without a mark was not reached. (b) Share of P/LP receiving any pathogenic evidence and of B/LB receiving any benign evidence.

**Figure 4. Discrimination on experimental splicing assays (Part 2).**
AUROC difference, AVI minus (a) SpliceAI raw, (b) SpliceAI masked, (c) Pangolin, (d) CADD, per dataset (squares, area proportional to the random-effects weight; 95% intervals from a variant bootstrap for single-gene datasets and a gene bootstrap otherwise). Number of variants in brackets. Diamond: random-effects pooled estimate over the main datasets with its DerSimonian-Laird 95% interval; line through it: Hartung-Knapp 95% interval; k, number of datasets; I², heterogeneity. Grey: comparison sets, not pooled (MFASS; OpenSplice variants at AVI developers' benchmark positions). Pangolin was run on the four single-exon datasets.

**Figure 5. Part 2 by position relative to the exon (additional analysis).**
Pooled AUROC difference, AVI minus each tool, for exonic variants, intronic variants 3–50 bases from the exon, and intronic variants more than 50 bases from the exon (the Part 1 deep intronic cut). A dataset's class is included when it holds at least 10 splice-altering and 10 other variants; k is the number of datasets (Pangolin / other tools). The >50-base class has two datasets (COMPASS and OpenSplice), so its Hartung-Knapp intervals are wide, and Pangolin (single-exon datasets only) is absent. Thick lines: DerSimonian-Laird 95% interval; thin lines: Hartung-Knapp 95% interval.

**Figure S1. SpliceAI2 (comparator added after the main results).**
(a) Part 1, AUROC difference AVI minus SpliceAI2 by region. (b) Part 2 per dataset and pooled over the five main datasets; OpenSplice, on which the SpliceAI2 developers evaluated their model, is shown separately and not pooled. (c) Part 2 by position relative to the exon (OpenSplice not pooled). Score: the SpliceAI2 summary score (precomputed v2.0).

**Figure S2. Missense CADD thresholds applied to intronic variants (additional analysis).**
The CADD score intervals recommended for missense variants (Pejaver et al. 2022, Table 2) evaluated as likelihood ratios on the intronic and UTR variants of the main set; bars are the ClinGen targets for each interval's evidence level; right, the share of P/LP and of B/LB falling in each interval. The intronic thresholds derived in this study are given in Supplementary Table [x].
